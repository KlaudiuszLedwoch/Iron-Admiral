Short summary field: DIP "The Great Game" with a battle AI that fights: torpedo charges, no free kills on slower ships, national doctrines, stronger minors, balance-of-power events. One-click install, full uninstall, MelonLoader bundled.

==========================================================================

[b]One-click install. Full uninstall. Everything bundled (MelonLoader, TweaksAndFixes, DIP data, source code).[/b]

If you liked the Dreadnought Improvement Project but felt the AI still plays like a script, this is for you. Iron Admiral keeps DIP's map, nations and data and adds a small MelonLoader add-on (DIP_GG_Fixes) that changes how the AI behaves in battle and in the campaign — things no CSV edit can do.

[h2]Battle AI[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Torpedo charges.[/b] AI destroyers, torpedo boats and light cruisers charge your battleships and heavy cruisers to deliver torpedoes, then withdraw when empty or badly hit.
[*][b]No more free kills.[/b] Any AI division that cannot outrun your pursuit stops fleeing and turns to fight. If it is faster, it keeps running — as it should.
[*][b]Cripples don't slow the fleet.[/b] Damaged, slow ships are detached and retreat on their own; the healthy ships keep fighting at full speed.
[*][b]Focus fire.[/b] AI guns prefer targets that are already damaged.
[*][b]National doctrines.[/b] Each nation fights by its historical doctrine (Royal Navy: long range and line; Japan: torpedo charges from 16 km, losses accepted; Italy: fights only with an advantage; Germany: torpedo flotillas cover the turn-away). Based on the Grand Fleet Battle Orders, the USN War Instructions and the Kaisen Yōmurei literature. All in one editable CSV.
[/list]

[h2]Campaign AI and world[/h2]
[list]
[*]AI nations that cannot afford a battleship build cruisers instead of leaving their yards empty.
[*]Balance of power: when your fleet outgrows the strongest rival by half, AI nations get emergency naval funding.
[*]Sovereign minor nations get their territories back and behave like in vanilla again: alliances, ship purchases, wars. Stronger minor defence, doubled minor port capacity for the warship trade.
[*]Diplomacy with consequences: improvements x1.5, worsening x4 — for you and for the AI.
[*]Post-war crisis softened so nations don't spiral into permanent bankruptcy.
[*]Your campaign designs are automatically exported to the shared design pool.
[*]Two game-code bugs that aborted turn processing in DIP ("An item with the same key has already been added") are caught, so turns complete.
[/list]

[h2]Historical accuracy and battleship design freedom[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Armour penetration from physics:[/b] De Marre formula for all 21 calibres and 5 gun generations, calibrated on six documented penetration tables (US 8"/55, German 28 cm, US 12"/50, British 13.5"/45 and 15"/42, US 16"/50 Mk 7), 0.5–13% error. Every calibre is based on a real gun. DIP scaled penetration linearly with calibre.
[*][b]Gun weights:[/b] barrel weight W = 0.05564·D^2.7732 t, calibrated on the 16"/50 Mk 7, 15"/42 Mk I and Yamato's 46 cm.
[*][b]Naval treaties, modelled for the first time:[/b] Washington 35,000 t from the 1919 hull tech, Second London escalator 45,000 t from 1936, treaties lapse in 1940. DIP had no battleship cap at all.
[*][b]Design freedom after 1940:[/b] the 14 Super Battleship hulls go from DIP's 78,500–130,000 t to 200,000–240,000 t (bb_8_china 200,000 … bb_7_bismarck 240,000), hard ceiling 240,000 t instead of infinity. Hull steel was never the limit — docks were; H-44 (~131,000 t) was judged unbuildable in the 1940s. Here you get the docks.
[*][b]Unbuildable hulls fixed:[/b] 29 Modern Battleship variants (Iowa, Bismarck, Richelieu, KGV, Littorio, Soyuz…) unlocked at 42,500–66,000 t minimum under a 35,000–45,000 t cap — dead techs. Now 35,000 t minimum (closer to history: South Dakota ~37,000, KGV ~36,000, Bismarck ~41,700). Plus 23 earlier BB hulls, 5 permanently unbuildable destroyer hulls and a corrupted CSV row that dropped every modern-BB unlock.
[*][b]Long Lance:[/b] +100% range for oxygen torpedoes (Type 93: 40,000 m vs ~12,000–13,700 m for everyone else). [b]Radar:[/b] night visibility +20/35/50 and bad-weather accuracy, which DIP never granted.
[/list]

[h2]Ships and tech (short list)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Armour penetration rebuilt from physics (De Marre) for 21 calibres and 5 gun generations; barrel weights calibrated on real guns.
[*][b]Naval treaties:[/b] DIP had no battleship tonnage cap. Now: Washington Treaty 35,000 t (from 1919 tech), Second London escalator 45,000 t (1936), treaties lapse in 1940 — and then the Super Battleship tier is raised from 78,500–130,000 t to 200,000–240,000 t per hull, 240,000 t hard ceiling instead of infinity.
[*]Fixed hulls that could never be built in DIP (Modern/Super Battleship, several BB and DD hulls) and a corrupted CSV row blocking modern battleship unlocks.
[*]Radar finally helps at night and in bad weather. Long Lance torpedoes: +100% range.
[*]Research ~20% faster, national tech specialisations x1.5, new Byzantine Empire profile.
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
Unzip anywhere and double-click [b]Install.bat[/b] — a readable PowerShell script, no exe. It backs up your current Mods folder (Uninstall.bat restores it), downloads the official MelonLoader 0.6.6 from the LavaGang GitHub release and verifies its SHA-256 before extracting, then installs the mod. Internet is needed during install and on the first launch, which takes a few minutes. Start a new campaign.

[h2]Transparency[/h2]
Every change is documented with reasoning, sources and test status in CHANGELOG_EN.txt (33 sections). Everything in the add-on has a switch in MelonPreferences.cfg. Source code included (Apache 2.0). Extension by Klaudiusz Ledwoch (Steam: Klod76). Built on DIP (Brother Munro & team, Apache 2.0), TweaksAndFixes (NathanKell, MIT) and MelonLoader (LavaGang, Apache 2.0).

[h2]Files[/h2]
Main file: Iron_Admiral_1.1_Nexus.zip — extract anywhere and run Install.bat (a readable PowerShell script, no exe). Optional file: Iron_Admiral_1.1_Mods_only.zip — just the Mods folder, for those who already have MelonLoader 0.6.6 set up (replace your Mods folder with it). Based on DIP The Great Game 1.3.5; TweaksAndFixes 3.21.1 is bundled. MelonLoader 0.6.6 is [b]not[/b] bundled: the installer downloads the official MelonLoader.x64.zip from the LavaGang GitHub release and verifies its SHA-256 (687b8260…3c0db) before extracting, so internet access is needed during installation.

Source code (DIP_GG_Fixes, installer, merge scripts) and both zips: [url=https://github.com/KlaudiuszLedwoch/Iron-Admiral]https://github.com/KlaudiuszLedwoch/Iron-Admiral[/url]

Jeśli lubisz Dreadnought Improvement Project, ale AI nadal gra jak skrypt — to jest dla ciebie. Iron Admiral zachowuje mapę, nacje i dane DIP, a dodaje mały dodatek pod MelonLoadera (DIP_GG_Fixes), który zmienia zachowanie AI w bitwie i w kampanii — rzeczy, których nie da się zrobić edycją CSV.

[h2]AI bitewne[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Szarże torpedowe.[/b] Niszczyciele, torpedowce i lekkie krążowniki AI idą na twoje pancerniki i ciężkie krążowniki, odpalają torpedy i wycofują się, gdy ich zabraknie albo są mocno uszkodzone.
[*][b]Koniec darmowych zatopień.[/b] Każdy dywizjon AI, który nie jest w stanie uciec przed pościgiem, przestaje uciekać i odwraca się do walki. Jeśli jest szybszy, ucieka dalej — jak powinien.
[*][b]Kaleki nie spowalniają floty.[/b] Uszkodzone, wolne okręty są odłączane i wycofują się osobno; sprawne walczą dalej z pełną prędkością.
[*][b]Skupianie ognia.[/b] Działa AI wybierają cele już uszkodzone.
[*][b]Doktryny narodowe.[/b] Każda nacja walczy według własnej doktryny historycznej (Royal Navy: dystans i linia; Japonia: szarże torpedowe z 16 km, akceptacja strat; Włochy: walka tylko przy przewadze; Niemcy: flotylle torpedowe osłaniają odwrót). Oparte na Grand Fleet Battle Orders, War Instructions USN i literaturze o Kaisen Yōmurei. Wszystko w jednym edytowalnym CSV.
[/list]

[h2]AI kampanii i świat[/h2]
[list]
[*]Nacje AI, których nie stać na pancernik, budują krążowniki zamiast stać z pustą stocznią.
[*]Równowaga sił: gdy twoja flota przerasta najsilniejszego rywala o połowę, nacje AI dostają nadzwyczajne środki na marynarkę.
[*]Suwerenne państwa minor odzyskują terytoria i znów działają jak w vanilla: sojusze, zakupy okrętów, wojny. Silniejsza obrona minorów, podwojona pojemność portów w handlu okrętami.
[*]Dyplomacja z konsekwencjami: poprawa x1,5, pogorszenie x4 — dla ciebie i dla AI.
[*]Złagodzony kryzys po przegranej wojnie, żeby państwa nie wpadały w wieczne bankructwo.
[*]Twoje projekty z kampanii trafiają automatycznie do puli projektów współdzielonych.
[*]Dwa błędy kodu gry przerywające turę w DIP ("An item with the same key has already been added") są przechwytywane — tury się kończą.

[h2]Instalacja[/h2]
Rozpakuj gdziekolwiek i kliknij dwukrotnie [b]Install.bat[/b] — czytelny skrypt PowerShell, bez exe. Internet jest potrzebny w trakcie instalacji i przy pierwszym uruchomieniu, które trwa kilka minut. Zacznij nową kampanię.
