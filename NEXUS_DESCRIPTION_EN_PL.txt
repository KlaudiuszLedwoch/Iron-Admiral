[size=4][b]What is new in 1.2.77 (8 November 2026)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Aircraft carriers[/b] - own ship type CV and ready historical designs for every nation and era. In the campaign a carrier at sea supports battles within its aircraft's reach (225 km in 1929 to 300 km in 1942), reveals enemy forces within 370 km, and replaces lost aircraft like ammunition. In battle it keeps out of gun range and fights with its aircraft.
[*][b]Task force orders[/b] - Patrol, Raid, Shadow, Intercept, Bombard, Blockade on the campaign map, from the tags, the tab strip or a right-click. Convoy escorts attack the pursuer while the convoy scatters.
[*][b]Diplomacy[/b] - war weariness, peace treaties that bind for 5 years, peace returns the war's conquests, naval treaties to sign or refuse, at most two wars, the Ottoman Empire with its own flag and ship names.
[*][b]Economy and armies[/b] - historical economic growth, true monthly balance, army budget, armies follow the economy and their supply lines, invasions sized by historical armies, coal and oil deposits.
[*][b]Ships[/b] - 94 new historical classes, historical build times and refits, crews learn at sea, flooding lists and capsizes ships, lighter torpedo launchers.
[*][b]Shared designs[/b] with their battle record and a design ranking; F10 laid out as ministries; loading-screen tips; beta updates on by default (F10).
[/list]

[size=4][b]What is new in 1.2.76 (1 November 2026)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Naval aviation[/b] - a new technology branch with its own budget: air reconnaissance from your ports, spotting aircraft, and torpedo air strikes in campaign battles from carriers and from the shore - all following research. Custom battles 1915-1945 give you a carrier beyond the horizon.
[*][b]Fog of war[/b] - the sea beyond your reconnaissance is delicately darkened on the campaign map; in battle an enemy not yet identified shows only a grey reported class, e.g. [i]CL?[/i].
[*][b]Diplomacy and the naval arms race[/b] - a real building programme alarms your rivals and they answer by building; a monthly report tells you who. Scrapping capital ships without growing their tonnage improves relations. A "Naval rivals" panel shows who sees whom as a rival.
[*][b]Battles[/b] - the faster force chooses the hour, night actions at short range, AI withdrawal and smoke, new orders (Smoke, Keep range, torpedo attack), no more ships outrunning their own torpedoes, one-sided battles no longer end as draws.
[*][b]Designs[/b] - historical AP shells, new AI designs with the newest researched shell, a service record and ranking for every design, deleted designs stay deleted.
[*][b]Much shorter end of turn[/b] - foreign navies design their ships while you plan your turn.
[/list]

[b]In the beta (1.2.77-beta.2):[/b] gun range and rate of fire consistent across calibres, no 30,000 t battleship hulls before 1912, air strike notices before and after a battle, the naval aviation of every great power.

[size=4][b]What the mod does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Battle AI that fights[/b] - torpedo attacks by range and speed, capital ships that close in or break off, divisions with orders, crews that matter (experience, panic, surrender), prizes taken from surrendered ships, national doctrines by period.
[*][b]Ballistics and damage from history[/b] - penetration, shell weights and muzzle velocities after historical guns; ships sink by their size; armour quality and impact angle count.
[*][b]A living campaign[/b] - treasury with loans and inflation, series production and shipyards, occupation, risings and garrisons, coalition wars against a dominant power, technology spread, stronger minor nations.
[*][b]Fog of war[/b] - enemy fleets known only as well as your reconnaissance allows, misidentified ships, ageing contact reports.
[*][b]Historical ship classes[/b] - hundreds of historical designs for the AI and for you, plus the Shippack designs.
[*][b]Settings panel (F10)[/b] - every feature has its own switch, with help texts in the game.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Download the installer zip from [url=https://github.com/KlaudiuszLedwoch/Iron-Admiral/releases]GitHub releases[/url] (or the Nexus file).
[*]Unzip it and right-click [i]Install-IronAdmiral.ps1[/i] > Run with PowerShell. It installs .NET 6, the VC++ Redistributable, MelonLoader 0.6.6 and Iron Admiral.
[*]Start the game. Updates are offered in the game; the beta line can be switched on in F10.
[/list]
Manual installation and uninstallation are described on the wiki: [url=https://github.com/KlaudiuszLedwoch/Iron-Admiral/wiki/Installation]Installation[/url].
