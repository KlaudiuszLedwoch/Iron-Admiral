# Iron Admiral roadmap

_Updated 22 November 2026 - latest beta 1.2.78-beta.550, stable 1.2.77.9. Bug fixes always go first. The same list is kept on Discord (#roadmap), in the wiki (Development plan) and in GitHub issues._

## Now (beta)
- **Notification filter for the game's own messages.** Small items (relations between other nations, new AI designs and ships, your turn reports, wars, alliances and peace) go to a feed at the side of the screen for 30 seconds instead of a window to click; F10 chooses which kinds still get a window. Beta.548 already added "Don't show this window again today" to the mod's own windows.
- **Standing relation orders.** Per nation: keep improving or keep worsening relations every turn until switched off; paused while not possible.
- **Battle AI as my own layer.** The game's battle AI freezes on conflicting decisions ("duck shooting"); I am covering it with my own decision layer: torpedo charges by the whole group, launch range and salvo timing by losses, firing on the last good chance, no idling under fire.
- **Performance.** Campaign map start of month (#494) and battle effects done in betas 532-547; more as measured.

## Next, in this order
1. **Designer fixes** - the citadel note moves to a corner with a close button (#500); deck torpedo tubes on capital ships can explode when hit, multi-gun turret reload by historical figures (#501).
2. **Admirals** - first my own admiral (Klod76): enemy AI in battle and the autonomy of the player's divisions follow his profile; stylised portrait on the fleet card, the pre-battle screen and the dispatch log.
3. **Player profiles from battle logs** - a tool that builds a player's tactical profile from his battles; the base for admirals of other players (portraits only with consent).
4. **Carriers, submarines and mines finished** - submarines diving and fighting in battle, then building them in the campaign (#470).
5. **Citadel and armour layers** - plate by plate instead of fixed multipliers (#116), buoyancy, longitudinal bulkheads.
6. **Economy that follows history** - coal and oil fields apart (#197), oil logistics, faster campaign start after 1890 (#194).
7. **Technology tree as one piece** (#363).

## Later
- **PBEM multiplayer** - each player fights an AI with the other player's personality; the two results are averaged into one "as if" battle between them.
- Governments that set goals and decide on war, together with admirals.
- War phases and a variable time scale on the campaign map (#191).
- Fleet orders on the campaign map, sea control and blockades.
- Naval intelligence: silhouettes, intent, intelligence network (#17).
- Historical scenarios and campaign starts; Ottoman Empire splitting into Turkey and Greece.
- Converting one existing battleship or cruiser into a carrier.
- F10 choices kept across updates, once the mod settles (until then every beta starts from defaults on purpose).
