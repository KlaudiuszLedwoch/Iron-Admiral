Iron Admiral - aircraft models (Mods\IronAdmiral_Models)

The models are simplified for the game: node transforms baked, small parts removed, meshes reduced (fast quadric
mesh reduction), textures reduced to 512 px, back faces added. They are converted copies, not the originals.
No endorsement by the original authors is implied.
